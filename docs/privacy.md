# Recc Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Martins Osazuwa Osakue**, an independent Nigerian music producer publishing the app under the name **Recc Studio**, handles information when you use Recc, a mobile recording and mixing studio (“Recc” or the “app”). Recc Studio is the app/brand name; it is not a registered company. Privacy and support contact: **reccstudio@gmail.com**.

## Information Recc handles

- **Account and profile information:** email address, account identifiers, artist name, optional bio, genres, optional location, a local profile-photo file path, whether you are open to collaborations, and timestamps/version showing acceptance of the Terms and adult self-attestation. Supabase provides account authentication and cloud profile storage. The profile photo image stays on your device; its local path is stored in your private account profile. When you switch on “Open to collabs,” your artist name, bio, genres, and location are displayed in the artist directory to app users; your email and photo path are not included in that directory.
- **Audio, projects, and lyrics:** recordings, imported audio, project data, exports, and lyrics or notes you enter. The studio stores local projects and lyrics on your device. Collaboration features transmit a listening copy and collaborator recordings to Recc’s Supabase backend and Cloudflare R2 object storage when you choose to use those features. Shared mix presets you publish are stored so other users can find and use them.
- **Collaboration and community information:** invites, shared mix presets, mix-use events, reports you submit, blocked-account identifiers, and related account identifiers and timestamps needed to operate collaboration, moderation, and account deletion.
- **Push notifications:** if you allow notifications, Recc uses Firebase Cloud Messaging and a device notification token to deliver collaboration notices. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.
- **Diagnostics and service data:** Supabase, Firebase Cloud Messaging, the operating system, and app stores may process technical information needed to provide, secure, and troubleshoot their services. The current app dependency manifest includes Supabase and Firebase Core/Messaging and does not include an analytics or advertising SDK. This may change if new SDKs are added.

Recc requests microphone access when you record. Microphone audio is used for recording and related audio features. The app should only record after you start a recording action. Audio saved locally is not uploaded merely because it exists on your device; cloud transfer occurs when you use collaboration or sharing features that require it.

## How information is used

We use this information to provide recording, editing, export, account, artist discovery, collaboration, shared presets, notifications, security, moderation, support, and account deletion. Recc does not use recordings or lyrics for advertising. If advertising or paid purchase providers are added in a future release, this policy and the app-store disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled.

## Sharing and service providers

We share information with providers that operate features you request, including Supabase for authentication, database, and server functions; Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications; and Cloudflare R2 for collaboration files. The Supabase project is in West EU (Ireland). Cloudflare R2 and provider service data may be processed in other locations where those providers operate. Google Play or Apple may process transaction information if real in-app purchases are enabled in a future release. Recc does not sell personal information.

When you publish a mix preset, its name, description, author display name, preset settings, and use count are visible to Recc users. The artist directory is accessible through the app's public database API, so directory fields should be treated as publicly accessible on the internet. Do not publish content or profile details you do not want disclosed.

## Storage, security, and retention

We use access controls and encrypted network connections provided by our app/backend services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Local projects and lyrics remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app, subject to your device backups. Collaboration audio is removed when the recipient downloads it, when a user deletes the account and associated collaboration data, or when we otherwise delete it. Collaboration links expire after 15 minutes. Unclaimed collaboration files and provider backups may remain for a limited period while cleanup or backup cycles complete; contact us if you need a specific deletion request. Shared presets remain visible until unpublished, removed by the app operator after review, or deleted with the author’s account. Account and profile information is kept while the account is active and deleted when the account is deleted, subject to backups, security logs, and records we must retain by law. We retain moderation reports and block records only as needed to review abuse reports, enforce these Terms, prevent repeat abuse, and comply with law. Exact infrastructure backup purge periods are controlled by our service providers.

## Your choices and rights

You can edit profile details, remove shared presets, control microphone and notification permissions in device settings, and request account deletion in the app. You can also request deletion at **https://plfxrjuvffwuxszyrgxf.supabase.co/functions/v1/delete-account**. Account deletion removes cloud account data and collaboration files covered by the deletion process; local recordings and projects on your device remain there and must be deleted separately. For privacy requests or questions, contact **reccstudio@gmail.com**. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port your personal data, and to complain to a regulator.

## Children

Recc is intended only for adults aged **18 or older**. The app asks users to confirm they are 18 or older before entering and again before creating an account. The first confirmation is saved on your device; the account confirmation timestamp is saved with your Supabase profile. We do not knowingly permit users under 18 to use Recc. If you believe a minor has provided information, contact **reccstudio@gmail.com** so we can review and take appropriate action. These are age self-attestations; we do not collect a date of birth through these screens.

## International users and changes

Recc is intended to be available worldwide. Information may be processed in the countries where our providers operate, which may be outside your country. We will update this policy when our practices change and update the effective date. Where required, we will give notice and obtain consent before new processing begins.
