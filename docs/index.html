Recc is a mobile recording and mixing studio for artists who want to capture a thought, shape a track, and bring collaborators into the process.

Record the moment

Capture vocals and musical ideas right from your phone, then keep building on your project.

Shape your mix

Arrange audio, explore sounds and effects, and export a mix when it feels ready.

Create together

Share projects with collaborators and discover mix ideas from the Recc community.

Recc is designed for adults aged 18 and over. Your local recordings stay on your device unless you choose a sharing or collaboration feature. Read our privacy policy for details about information and cloud features.
